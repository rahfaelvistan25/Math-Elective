# Math Elective: Calculus for Business
## Module 1 – Business Functions & Limits

An interactive, responsive educational website covering the complete **Module 1: Business Functions & Limits** curriculum for Calculus for Business students.

---

### 🌟 Features Included

1. **Complete Verbatim Curriculum (Parts 1 to 14)**:
   - **Section I**: Introduction to the Module, Bakery Scenario (6 core questions), Learning Objectives.
   - **Part 1**: Understanding Business Functions, Definitions, Tables, Formulas, Symbols.
   - **Part 2**: Cost Functions (Fixed vs. Variable, Linear & Quadratic Nonlinear Models).
   - **Part 3**: Revenue Functions (Linear Price vs. Nonlinear Price-Demand Models).
   - **Part 4**: Profit Functions ($\pi(x) = R(x) - C(x)$, Linear & Quadratic Profit Optimization).
   - **Part 5**: Demand and Supply Functions (Laws of Demand & Supply, Linear & Nonlinear Equations).
   - **Part 6**: Market Equilibrium ($Q_d = Q_s$, Solving Linear & Quadratic Equilibrium).
   - **Part 7**: Break-Even Analysis ($R(x) = C(x)$, Contribution Margin, Multi-point Nonlinear Break-Even).
   - **Part 8**: Introduction to Limits ($\lim_{x \to a} f(x)$, Numeric Approach Tables).
   - **Part 9**: Evaluating Limits (Direct Substitution, Rational Functions, Factoring $\frac{0}{0}$, One-Sided Limits, Limits at Infinity & Average Cost).
   - **Part 10**: Continuity in Business Models (3 Continuity Conditions, Piecewise Pricing, Discontinuities).
   - **Part 11**: Summary Matrices & Master Formula Cheatsheet.
   - **Part 12**: 14 Practice Exercises with Interactive "Reveal Solution" toggles.
   - **Part 13**: Complete Answer Key with Step-by-Step Solutions.
   - **Part 14**: Final Review, Key Takeaways, and Suggested 6-Step Study Guide.

2. **Interactive Break-Even Simulator & Graph**:
   - Dynamic sliders for Fixed Cost ($F$), Variable Cost ($v$), and Selling Price ($p$).
   - Live Chart.js graph rendering Total Revenue $R(x)$, Total Cost $C(x)$, Fixed Cost $F$, and Break-Even Point $Q^*$.
   - Interactive visual indication of Loss and Profit zones.
   - Built-in presets: Burger Business (Ex. 13), Textbook Demo ($F=600, v=15, p=35$), and Bakery (Ex. 5).

3. **Self-Scoring Knowledge Check Quiz**:
   - 8 interactive multiple-choice questions with real-time feedback, detailed explanations, dynamic progress count ("X of 8 answered"), and score tally.
   - Confetti animation upon achieving a high score.

4. **One-Page Infographic Poster (Print & Digital)**:
   - High-density visual infographic synthesizing the entire module across 6 themed panels:
     1. Cost Structure ($C(x) = F + vx$)
     2. Revenue Dynamics ($R(x) = px$)
     3. Profit & Break-Even ($Q^* = \frac{F}{p-v}$)
     4. Market Equilibrium ($Q_d = Q_s$)
     5. Limits in Business ($\lim_{x \to a} f(x)$)
     6. Continuity Checklist (3 Tests)
   - Features a **Print / Save as PDF** button with `@media print` CSS optimized for a clean 1-page cheatsheet poster.
   - Fullscreen presentation mode.

5. **Student Experience & Accessibility**:
   - KaTeX high-speed LaTeX rendering for mathematical typography.
   - Dark / Light mode toggle with persistent state.
   - Reading progress bar and scroll-spy Table of Contents with quick search filter.
   - Quick Formula Cheatsheet popup modal.

# Math Elective: Calculus for Business
## Module 1 – Business Functions & Limits (Beginner Edition)

An interactive, responsive educational website covering the beginner-friendly reorganized **Module 1: Business Functions & Limits** curriculum.

---

### 🌟 Features of the Updated Lesson

1. **Reorganized Beginner-Friendly Structure (Parts 1 to 18)**:
   - **Part 1: Introduction to Business Functions**: Simple bottled water example, total sales, $R(x) = 20x$.
   - **Part 2: Linear Cost Function**: Fixed vs. Variable costs, word problem clue identifier table, $C(x) = F + vx$, notebook examples with step-by-step breakdown.
   - **Part 3: Nonlinear Cost Function**: Explanation of increasing marginal costs, $C(x) = ax^2 + bx + F$, $x^2$ review, and worked example.
   - **Part 4: Revenue Function**: $R(x) = px$, word problem clue identifier table, bags example, changing price function $p(x) = 100 - 2x$.
   - **Part 5: Profit Function**: $P(x) = R(x) - C(x)$, positive/zero/negative profit, keyboard shop example, profit vs. loss explanation.
   - **Part 6: Demand Function**: Law of demand, $Q_d = a - bp$, milk tea demand example, solving for quantity and price.
   - **Part 7: Supply Function**: Law of supply, $Q_s = c + dp$, bottle water supply example, solving for quantity and price.
   - **Part 8: Market Equilibrium**: Demand = Supply ($Q_d = Q_s$), step-by-step equilibrium price and quantity solution.
   - **Part 9: Break-Even Analysis**: $Q = \frac{F}{p - v}$, milk tea business example, profit/loss breakdown table (50, 100, 120 products), and **embedded interactive simulator**.
   - **Part 10: Introduction to Limits**: Numerical approach table ($f(x) = 2x+3$ approaching 4), limit notation $\lim_{x \to 4} (2x+3) = 11$.
   - **Part 11: Evaluating Limits by Direct Substitution**: 4-step direct substitution method, 3 step-by-step worked examples.
   - **Part 12: Limits Resulting in Zero Divided by Zero (0/0)**: Indeterminate forms, factoring $x^2 - 9 = (x-3)(x+3)$, canceling factors, and re-substituting.
   - **Part 13: One-Sided Limits**: Left-hand ($\lim_{x \to a^-}$) and right-hand ($\lim_{x \to a^+}$) limits, delivery fee piecewise rates example.
   - **Part 14: Continuity in Business Functions**: 3 conditions for continuity, continuous vs. discontinuous delivery fee examples.
   - **Part 15: Quick Formula Review**: Master formula matrix and symbol guide.
   - **Part 16: How to Solve Business Word Problems**: The 5-Step Beginner Problem-Solving Framework (Read, Find Clues, Choose Formula, Substitute, Calculate with Units).
   - **Part 17: Student Practice Exercises**: 16 categorized practice exercises covering all 9 core topics with interactive **"Reveal Solution"** toggles.
   - **Part 18: Answer Key with Basic Solutions**: Complete step-by-step solutions for all 16 practice problems.
   - **Final Summary for Students**: 9 simple ideas, key formulas to memorize, study tips, and guided worksheet notes.

2. **Interactive Break-Even Simulator & Dynamic Graph**:
   - Interactive sliders for **Fixed Cost ($F$)**, **Variable Cost ($v$)**, and **Selling Price ($p$)**.
   - Presets for the Milk Tea Example ($F=3000, v=50, p=80$), Practice Problem #10 ($F=4000, v=60, p=100$), and Notebook Biz ($F=2000, v=30, p=70$).
   - Dynamic Chart.js graph plotting Revenue $R(x)$, Total Cost $C(x)$, Fixed Cost $F$, and Break-Even Point ($Q$), highlighting Loss and Profit zones.

3. **Self-Checking Interactive Quiz**:
   - 8 beginner-friendly questions with real-time feedback, detailed explanations, and score tracking with celebratory confetti.

4. **One-Page Infographic Poster**:
   - Synthesizes the beginner-friendly framework across 6 color-coded panels with `@media print` layout and fullscreen presentation mode.
